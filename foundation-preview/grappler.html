HOW THE GRAPPLER™ WORKS

1APPROACH

The officer positions the equipped police vehicle within the trained deployment range.

2DEPLOY

The front-mounted Grappler™ deploys its heavy-duty straps toward the fleeing vehicle's rear wheel.

3CONTROL

Once engaged, trained officers use the system to help bring the fleeing vehicle to a controlled stop.
