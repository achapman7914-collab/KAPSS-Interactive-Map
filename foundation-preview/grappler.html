Why it matters

A pursuit can change lives in seconds.

Vehicle pursuits can place officers, fleeing drivers and uninvolved motorists in rapidly changing situations. The Grappler Police Bumper is a vehicle-mounted immobilization system that deploys a heavy-duty net around a target vehicle's rear wheel assembly.

It gives trained officers an additional pursuit-intervention option. The goal is not to claim that any intervention eliminates risk, but to provide another tool that may help officers bring an appropriate pursuit to an earlier, more controlled conclusion.

How it works

A different approach to vehicle intervention.

01 • APPROACH

Position

The specially equipped police vehicle moves into the trained deployment position behind the target vehicle.

02 • DEPLOY

Capture

The system deploys netting toward the rear wheel area, where it is designed to entangle the wheel, axle or suspension components.

03 • CONTROL

Bring it to an end

Once engaged, the trained officer uses the system and police vehicle to help control and immobilize the fleeing vehicle.

Community impact

Another option when seconds matter.

PUBLIC

Designed to give trained officers another option for ending dangerous fleeing-vehicle incidents.

OFFICERS

The system is deployed from an equipped police vehicle, allowing the officer to remain inside the vehicle during deployment.

CONTROL

Rather than intentionally spinning a vehicle, the system is designed to capture a rear wheel and assist in immobilizing the vehicle.

TRAINING

Deployment requires approved training, policy and agency procedures—not simply the purchase of equipment.
