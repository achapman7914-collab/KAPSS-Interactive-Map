Why it matters

A pursuit can change lives in seconds.

Vehicle pursuits can put officers, fleeing drivers, passengers, pedestrians and uninvolved motorists in rapidly changing situations. The Grappler Police Bumper is a vehicle-mounted intervention system designed to deploy a heavy-duty net around a target vehicle’s rear wheel area.

It gives trained officers an additional pursuit-intervention option. The purpose is not to claim that any intervention eliminates risk, but to provide another tool that may help bring an appropriate pursuit to an earlier, more controlled conclusion.

How it works

A different approach to vehicle intervention.

01 • APPROACH

Position

The specially equipped police vehicle moves into the trained deployment position behind the target vehicle.

02 • DEPLOY

Capture

The system deploys netting toward the rear wheel area, where it is designed to entangle the wheel, axle or suspension components.

03 • CONTROL

Bring it to an end

Once engaged, the trained officer uses the system and police vehicle to help control and immobilize the fleeing vehicle.
