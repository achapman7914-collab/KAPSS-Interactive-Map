How the Grappler™ Works

1APPROACH

The police vehicle moves into position behind the target vehicle.

2DEPLOY

The front-mounted Grappler webbing is deployed beneath the target vehicle.

3ENGAGE

The webbing engages the target vehicle’s rear wheel area so officers can work toward a controlled stop.
