Why Community Engagement Matters

Relationships built before a crisis make a stronger community.

Community engagement gives officers and residents more opportunities to know one another as neighbors and partners. Foundation funding can expand the events, programs and acts of service that strengthen trust, encourage dialogue and create meaningful connection throughout Jeffersontown.

Build TrustPositive interaction creates familiarity and stronger relationships.
Open DialogueInformal settings create space to listen, ask questions and understand one another.
Strengthen CommunityShared events and service reinforce that public safety works best as a partnership.
