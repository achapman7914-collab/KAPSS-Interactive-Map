Community Engagement

Building Trust Through Connection.

Strong communities are built through relationships. Foundation support can help Jeffersontown Police officers connect with residents outside of enforcement settings through positive, practical and family-centered outreach.

Supporting Officers as Community Partners.

Community engagement funding can help make outreach activities more accessible, welcoming and impactful. These efforts create opportunities for residents to know the officers who serve them, for children and families to engage with police in positive settings, and for JPD to give back to the community in meaningful ways.

Community Events

Support for National Night Out, neighborhood gatherings, Coffee or Donuts with a Cop, open houses and other events that create informal opportunities for conversation and connection.

Youth & Safety Programs

Resources for bicycle-safety courses, youth activities, school partnerships, safety education and family-focused events that strengthen trust from an early age.

Giving Back

Support for officer-led acts of service such as delivering holiday gifts to children in hospitals, assisting families during special events and participating in other community-centered charitable activities.

Help Strengthen the J-Town Connection.

Your support can help create more opportunities for officers and residents to meet, serve, learn and build trust together.
