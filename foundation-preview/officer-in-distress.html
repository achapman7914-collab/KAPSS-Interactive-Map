A Community That Stands With Its Own

Behind Every Badge Is a Person and a Family.

Serious illness, injury, loss and other catastrophic circumstances can create sudden emotional and financial strain. In those moments, practical support and a caring community can make a meaningful difference.

The Foundation's goal is to build a trusted source of assistance that can respond when a member of the JPD family is facing a significant and unexpected hardship.

Those who serve our community should know their community stands with them.
How Support May Help

Meeting Difficult Moments With Practical Help.

Assistance may be directed toward urgent needs created by serious or catastrophic circumstances.

＋

Serious Illness or Injury

Helping ease unexpected burdens during a major medical event, injury or extended recovery.

♡

Family Crisis or Loss

Providing compassionate support when a family is facing devastating loss or extraordinary personal hardship.

◇

Unexpected Financial Strain

Offering limited assistance for urgent needs when extraordinary circumstances create sudden financial burden.

Program details are being developed. Eligibility, application procedures, review standards and specific forms of assistance will be established by the Jeffersontown Police Foundation. This page describes the purpose of the program and does not guarantee assistance in any particular circumstance.
