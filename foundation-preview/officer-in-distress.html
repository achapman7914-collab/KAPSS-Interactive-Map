Officer & Family Support

When Those Who Serve Need Us Most.

The Jeffersontown Police Foundation exists to stand behind the people who stand behind our community. The Officer in Distress program is intended to provide compassionate support when a Jeffersontown Police Department employee or family is facing an extraordinary hardship.

Support Beyond the Uniform

A Community That Stands With Its Own.

Serious illness, injury, loss and other catastrophic circumstances can create sudden emotional and financial strain. In those moments, practical support and a caring community can make a meaningful difference.

The Foundation's goal is to build a trusted source of assistance that can respond when a member of the JPD family is facing a significant and unexpected hardship.

“
Those who serve our community should know their community stands with them.Private support gives the Foundation flexibility to respond with compassion and care when difficult circumstances arise.
How Support May Help

Meeting Difficult Moments With Practical Help.

As the Foundation develops this program, assistance may be directed toward urgent needs created by serious or catastrophic circumstances.

Area 01

Serious Illness or Injury

Helping ease unexpected burdens when a member of the JPD family is dealing with a major medical event, injury or extended recovery.

Area 02

Family Crisis or Loss

Providing compassionate support when a family is facing a devastating loss or another extraordinary personal hardship.

Area 03

Unexpected Financial Strain

Offering limited assistance for urgent needs when extraordinary circumstances create a sudden and significant financial burden.

Program details are being developed. Eligibility, application procedures, review standards and specific forms of assistance will be established by the Jeffersontown Police Foundation. This page describes the purpose of the program and does not guarantee assistance in any particular circumstance.
Why It Matters

Service. Family. Community.

The people who show up for Jeffersontown every day should know that Jeffersontown will show up for them when life becomes difficult. This program is one way the Foundation can turn community support into meaningful help when it matters most.

Standing Behind Those Who Serve.Honor, service and community are more than words. They are a promise that our JPD family will not face life's hardest moments alone.

Help Us Be There When It Matters.

Your support can help the Foundation build a resource that is ready to respond when a member of the Jeffersontown Police Department family is facing one of life's hardest moments.
