Officer in Distress

When Those Who Serve Need Us Most.

The Jeffersontown Police Foundation exists to stand behind the people who stand behind our community. The Officer in Distress program is intended to help provide compassionate support when a Jeffersontown Police Department employee or family is facing an extraordinary hardship.

Support Beyond the Uniform.

Serious illness, injury, loss and other catastrophic circumstances can create sudden emotional and financial strain. In those moments, community support can make a meaningful difference.

The Foundation's goal is to help create a trusted source of assistance that can respond when a member of the JPD family is facing a significant, unexpected hardship.

Those who serve our community should know their community stands with them.Private support can help the Foundation respond with compassion, flexibility and care when difficult circumstances arise.
Behind every badge is a person, a family and a life beyond the uniform.Officer in Distress is about standing together when unexpected hardship changes everything.
How Support May Help

Meeting Difficult Moments With Practical Help.

As the Foundation develops this program, assistance may be directed toward urgent needs created by serious or catastrophic circumstances.

Serious Illness or Injury

Helping ease unexpected burdens when a member of the JPD family is dealing with a major medical event or recovery.

Family Crisis or Loss

Providing compassionate support when a family is facing a devastating loss or extraordinary personal hardship.

Unexpected Financial Strain

Offering limited assistance for urgent needs when circumstances create a sudden and significant financial burden.

Program details are being developed. Eligibility, application procedures, review standards and specific forms of assistance will be established by the Jeffersontown Police Foundation. This page is intended to describe the purpose of the program and does not guarantee assistance in any particular circumstance.
