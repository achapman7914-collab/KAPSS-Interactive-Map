Community Connection

Stronger Relationships. A Stronger Jeffersontown.

Public safety is about more than responding to emergencies. It is about building relationships before an emergency ever happens—and being there with the right support when it does.

Through REACH and victim services, JPD helps people navigate crisis, victimization, mental-health and substance-use concerns by connecting individuals and families with support and resources that can help move them toward safety and stability.

National Night Out, D.A.R.E., youth outreach, prevention, education and community events create positive opportunities for officers and professional staff to connect with residents outside of a crisis, building trust, familiarity and communication.

When you support community engagement, you are investing in more than a program. You are investing in relationships—and those relationships help build a safer, stronger and more connected Jeffersontown.

Department Wellness

Supporting the People Behind the Badge.

The effects of a career in law enforcement do not always end when a shift is over—and they can affect the families who stand beside those who serve.

JPD's wellness approach supports mental, emotional and physical well-being, including access to confidential professional therapy at no cost and trauma-focused treatment such as EMDR. Support also recognizes that the effects of the profession can continue into retirement.

Professional Mental Health Care

Confidential therapy and trauma-focused care available at no cost.

Physical Wellness & Recovery

Fitness resources, gym access, wellness spaces and recovery initiatives such as cold plunge.

Peer & Resilience Support

Peer support, resilience resources and suicide-prevention education.

Families & Retirees

Recognizing that occupational stress can affect families and continue beyond active service.
