Our Mission

Strong officers.
Strong community.

The Jeffersontown Police Foundation supports the men and women of the Jeffersontown Police Department and strengthens the community they serve.

Through private donations, partnerships and fundraising, the Foundation helps support opportunities and needs beyond traditional public funding — from officer wellness and specialized training to technology, equipment and community programs.

What We Support

Support that makes a difference.

Focused investments that strengthen JPD, support officers and families, and build meaningful connections throughout Jeffersontown.
