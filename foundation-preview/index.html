Who We Are

Public Safety.
Powered by Partnership.

The Jeffersontown Police Foundation is an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit devoted to bolstering public safety in Jeffersontown and is the exclusive entity authorized to raise private funds for the Jeffersontown Police Department.

Our mission: To enhance the efficiency, preparedness and outreach of the Jeffersontown Police Department, helping ensure our community remains among the safest, most connected and welcoming places for residents, workers and visitors alike.

Our Vision
Public safety driven by innovation, built on trust and strengthened through community collaboration — positioning Jeffersontown as a model of proactive, community-focused policing.

Community Connection

Stronger Relationships.
A Stronger Jeffersontown.

Public safety is about more than responding to emergencies. It is about building relationships before an emergency ever happens—and being there with the right support when it does.

Through programs such as REACH and victim services, JPD helps individuals and families navigate some of life’s most difficult moments by connecting them with support, resources and services that can help move them toward safety and stability.

Initiatives such as National Night Out, D.A.R.E., youth outreach, prevention programs and community events give officers and professional staff opportunities to connect with residents outside of a crisis. Those positive interactions build familiarity, encourage communication and strengthen the trust that is essential to effective community policing.

When you support community engagement, you’re investing in more than a program. You’re investing in relationships—and those relationships help build a safer, stronger and more connected Jeffersontown.
Department Wellness

Supporting the People Behind the Badge.

Law enforcement professionals routinely encounter trauma, crisis, loss and high-stress situations as part of their work. The effects of that service do not always end when a shift is over—and they can affect the families who stand beside those who serve.

JPD’s wellness program takes a comprehensive approach to mental, emotional and physical well-being. Personnel have access to confidential professional therapy at no cost, including trauma-focused treatment such as EMDR. Wellness support also recognizes that the effects of a career in law enforcement can continue beyond active service, with support available for retirees as well.

Support Officer & Family Wellness
Professional Mental Health Care

Confidential therapy and trauma-focused treatment help ensure personnel can seek professional support without cost becoming a barrier.

Physical Wellness & Recovery

Fitness opportunities, an on-site gym, wellness and recovery spaces, and cold-plunge recovery support physical health and resilience.

Peer & Resilience Support

Peer support, resilience resources and suicide-prevention education help build a department culture where wellness is part of everyday readiness.

Families & Retirees

Wellness recognizes that the impact of the profession can extend beyond the individual officer and beyond active service.

Healthy officers strengthen healthy departments. Investing in wellness helps officers care for themselves, support their families, build resilience throughout their careers and continue serving the community at their best.
