Our Mission

Strong Officers.
Strong Community.

The Jeffersontown Police Foundation supports the men and women of the Jeffersontown Police Department and strengthens the community they serve.

Through private donations, partnerships and fundraising, the Foundation helps support opportunities beyond traditional public funding — including officer wellness, specialized training, technology, equipment and community programs.

What We Support

Support That Makes a Difference.

Officer Wellness

Mental, physical and family wellness resources that help officers remain healthy, resilient and ready to serve.

Training & Equipment

Specialized training, technology and equipment that improve readiness, effectiveness and officer safety.

Community Engagement & Outreach

Programs, partnerships, outreach and events that connect JPD with residents, families, youth and people needing support throughout Jeffersontown.

Public Safety Innovation

Forward-looking initiatives that give officers better information, tools and capabilities.

Featured Public-Safety Initiative

Drone as First Responder

Help bring next-generation emergency response technology to Jeffersontown.

The Foundation is preparing to support a right-sized DFR program designed specifically for Jeffersontown, including technology, infrastructure, implementation and ongoing program-management costs. Final costs will be added after the department completes its review.
