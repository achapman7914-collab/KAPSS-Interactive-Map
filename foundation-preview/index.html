Our Mission

Strong Officers.
Strong Community.

The Jeffersontown Police Foundation supports the men and women of the Jeffersontown Police Department and strengthens the community they serve.

Through private donations, partnerships and fundraising, the Foundation helps support needs beyond traditional public funding — including officer wellness, specialized training, technology, equipment and community programs.

What We Support

Support That Makes a Difference.

Officer Wellness

Mental, physical and family wellness resources that help officers remain healthy, resilient and ready to serve.

Training & Equipment

Specialized training, technology and equipment that improve readiness, effectiveness and officer safety.

Community Engagement

Youth outreach, education, events and partnerships that strengthen relationships throughout Jeffersontown.

Public Safety Innovation

Forward-looking initiatives that give JPD better information, tools and capabilities.
