Our Mission

Strong Officers.
Strong Community.

The Jeffersontown Police Foundation supports the men and women of the Jeffersontown Police Department and strengthens the community they serve.

Through private donations, partnerships and fundraising, the Foundation helps support opportunities beyond traditional public funding — including officer wellness, specialized training, technology, equipment, outreach and community programs.

Support That Makes a Difference.

Proudly Jeffersontown

Local Service. Local Impact.

The Foundation exists to strengthen the connection between the Jeffersontown community and the officers who serve it. The work is local, the partnerships are local, and the impact stays focused on Jeffersontown.

Featured Public-Safety Initiative

Drone as First Responder

Help bring next-generation emergency response technology to Jeffersontown.

The Foundation is preparing to support a right-sized Drone as First Responder program designed specifically for Jeffersontown. The campaign will support technology, infrastructure, implementation and ongoing annual program-management costs.

The department is still finalizing the proposed system configuration and cost. Those figures will be added here before the fundraising campaign is launched.
