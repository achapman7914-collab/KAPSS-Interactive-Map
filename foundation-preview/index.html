The Jeffersontown Police Foundation

Supporting ThoseWho Serve.

The Jeffersontown Police Foundation is an independent nonprofit dedicated to strengthening public safety by supporting the people, programs and tools that help the Jeffersontown Police Department serve our community at its best.

Our Mission in Action

We focus our efforts on four key pillars to support the Jeffersontown Police Department and strengthen the safety, wellness and effectiveness of those who protect and serve.

◎

Community Engagement

Supporting outreach, education, victim services, prevention and events.

★

Training & Equipment

Advancing specialized training and critical resources that help officers serve safely and effectively.

♥

Department Wellness

Investing in the mental, emotional and physical well-being of those who serve and their families.

✦

Innovation

Backing responsible technology and forward-thinking tools that strengthen public safety.
