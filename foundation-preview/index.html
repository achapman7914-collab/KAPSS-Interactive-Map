Supporting Those Who Serve

Private support helps strengthen public safety through essential resources, advanced equipment, officer wellness, meaningful community programs and forward-looking initiatives for the Jeffersontown Police Department.

Honor • Service • Community

The Jeffersontown Police Foundation is an independent nonprofit devoted to strengthening public safety and supporting the people, programs and tools that help JPD serve at its best.

★

Our Mission

Enhance the efficiency, preparedness and outreach of the Jeffersontown Police Department while helping ensure Jeffersontown remains a safe, connected and welcoming community.

◉

Our Vision

Public safety driven by innovation, built on trust and strengthened through community collaboration.

Our Four Strategic Pillars

How We Move the Mission Forward

Community Engagement

Supporting outreach, education, victim services, prevention and events that strengthen meaningful connections between JPD and the community.

Training & Equipment

Helping provide advanced tools, specialized training and critical resources that keep personnel prepared.

Department Wellness

Investing in mental, emotional and physical wellness resources for officers, families and retirees.

Innovation

Supporting proactive strategies and responsible technology that can strengthen awareness, response and community safety.
