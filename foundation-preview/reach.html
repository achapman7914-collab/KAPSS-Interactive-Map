Programs We Support • Community Care

REACH

Respond. Evaluate. Advocate. Connect. Heal.
REACH embeds social-services support within the Jeffersontown Police Department, helping connect people with practical assistance, advocacy and resources during some of life's most difficult moments.

The Jeffersontown Police Foundation can help strengthen REACH by supporting resources that allow the program to respond to victims, individuals experiencing mental-health or substance-use challenges, and families navigating crisis—while helping officers connect community members with specialized support beyond traditional law-enforcement response.

How REACH serves

Connecting people to help when it matters.

Victim Advocacy

Support, safety planning, court accompaniment, referrals and practical assistance for victims and families.

Mental Health & Recovery

Connection to behavioral-health, substance-use recovery and treatment resources, including follow-up after overdose.

Community Resources

Helping residents navigate transportation, emergency needs, housing resources, basic necessities and other community supports.

Officer Partnership

Giving officers a direct pathway to connect people with civilian social-services professionals for needs extending beyond enforcement.

Outreach & Prevention

Proactive follow-up, education and community partnerships designed to reduce barriers and connect people with services earlier.

Connection & Continuity

Helping individuals move from a moment of crisis toward longer-term community-based support and stability.

How Foundation Support Can Help

Private donations can help REACH respond with flexible resources that may not fit within traditional public-safety budgets—from emergency assistance and outreach materials to practical tools that help staff connect victims and vulnerable community members with services.
