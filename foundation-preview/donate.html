Current Initiative

Grappler

A specialized vehicle-mounted tool designed to give trained law-enforcement officers an additional option for safely bringing certain vehicle pursuits to an end.

Implementation remains subject to departmental review, policy, training and applicable approvals.

Current Initiative • Drone as First Responder

DFR Program

Six-Year Fundraising Goal$124,000

The proposed investment would provide a six-year Skydio X10 Drone as First Responder package, including the aircraft, software and services, parachute system, supporting equipment and related program technology.

DFR technology can help provide trained personnel with timely aerial information during appropriate public-safety responses and strengthen the department's existing drone capability.
