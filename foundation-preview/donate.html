Why Your Gift Matters

Turning Community Support Into Meaningful Action.

Some of the most important needs in public safety fall outside traditional government budgets or require additional support to move forward. The Foundation helps bridge that gap through private donations, partnerships and community investment.

Every contribution helps build a stronger network of support around the men and women of the Jeffersontown Police Department and the community they serve.

Your Gift Can Help

Strengthen officer wellness, support families in crisis, expand community programs, equip specialized teams and help fund responsible innovation.
