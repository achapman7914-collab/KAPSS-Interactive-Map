Meet the Team

Officer Crask & K9 Fonz

A working police K9 team is built through extensive training, discipline and trust. Handler and K9 work as one unit in situations where communication, control and precision are critical.

Officer Crask and Fonz represent that specialized partnership for the Jeffersontown Police Department. This page can be expanded with Fonz's breed, certifications, specific disciplines and service history as those details are added.

Two partners. One mission. Ready when called.
Working K9: Fonz is a trained law-enforcement K9, not a community or therapy dog. Members of the public should not approach, pet or attempt to handle him unless specifically directed by Officer Crask.
A Specialized Public Safety Resource

Built for the Work.

Working police K9 teams provide capabilities that require intensive training, continued proficiency and a strong handler partnership. Fonz's role is operational—supporting law-enforcement functions and officer safety when his specialized skills are needed.

◆

Narcotics Detection

Using specialized scent-detection training to assist officers with narcotics-related law-enforcement operations.

◎

Suspect Apprehension

Providing a trained K9 resource to assist officers in locating and apprehending suspects when deployment is appropriate.

✦

Search & Tracking

Applying specialized K9 abilities to searches and tracking tasks in support of law-enforcement operations.

◇

Officer Safety

Serving as an additional specialized resource during high-risk situations and other calls where K9 deployment can support officer safety.

Foundation Support

Helping Keep the Team Mission-Ready.

A working K9 requires significant ongoing investment throughout his career. Foundation support can help strengthen the program by providing resources that complement departmental funding and help Officer Crask and Fonz remain trained, equipped and ready for duty.

Specialized Training

Advanced handler and K9 training, continuing education, certifications and proficiency work required to maintain operational readiness.

K9 Equipment

Working leads, harnesses, training equipment and other specialized gear needed for safe and effective deployment.

Protective Equipment

Protective and safety equipment appropriate to Fonz's working role and operational needs.

Health & Veterinary Care

Resources that support the health, conditioning and veterinary needs of a working police K9 throughout his service career.

Vehicle & Kennel Needs

Specialized transportation, temperature-control, containment and kennel equipment designed for the safety of a working K9.

Long-Term K9 Readiness

Helping prepare for future program expenses, replacement equipment and other needs that sustain JPD's operational K9 capability.

Training. Control. Readiness.

A Working Partnership.

The effectiveness of a police K9 is inseparable from the relationship with the handler. Officer Crask and Fonz train as a team so commands, movement and deployment can be carried out with discipline and control.

Foundation support helps invest in the resources behind that readiness—from specialized training and working equipment to the care required to keep Fonz healthy throughout a demanding service career.
