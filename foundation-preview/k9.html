Meet the Team

Officer Crask & K9 Fonz

A police K9 team is built on far more than training. Handler and K9 work together, train together and rely on one another in situations where precision, communication and trust matter.

Officer Crask and Fonz represent that partnership for the Jeffersontown Police Department. As we develop this page, this section can be expanded with Fonz's breed, specialty, certifications, years of service and the story of how he and Officer Crask became a team.

Two partners. One mission. Serving Jeffersontown together.
A Specialized Public Safety Resource

What a K9 Team Brings to the Mission

K9 teams provide specialized capabilities while supporting officers during demanding calls for service. Their effectiveness depends on extensive training, a strong handler partnership and continued investment throughout the K9's working career.

◆

Specialized Detection

Supporting trained detection capabilities that can assist officers during appropriate law-enforcement operations.

◎

Officer Safety

Providing officers with an additional specialized resource during situations that may present elevated risk.

✦

Training & Readiness

Maintaining the skills, discipline and teamwork required of a dependable law-enforcement K9 team.

♡

Community Connection

Creating opportunities for residents to meet the team and learn more about the people and resources serving Jeffersontown.

Foundation Support

Helping Keep the Team Ready.

A working K9 requires ongoing investment throughout his career. Foundation support can help strengthen the program by providing resources that complement departmental funding and help the team remain equipped, trained and cared for.

Specialized Training

Advanced courses, continuing education, certifications and training opportunities for the handler and K9.

K9 Equipment

Specialized leads, harnesses, training tools and other equipment needed for safe and effective work.

Protective Equipment

Safety equipment and protective resources appropriate to the team's operational needs.

Health & Veterinary Care

Support for wellness, medical or other care needs when appropriate within the Foundation's program guidelines.

Vehicle & Kennel Needs

Specialized equipment and improvements that support safe transportation, housing and working conditions.

Future K9 Needs

Helping the Foundation prepare for major program expenses, future equipment and the long-term sustainability of JPD's K9 capability.

More Than a Working Team

A Connection With the Community.

K9 teams also offer a unique way for the public to connect with law enforcement. Community events, demonstrations and educational opportunities can help residents understand the training, responsibility and partnership behind the badge and the K9.

For the Foundation, supporting the K9 program is an opportunity to invest in public safety while giving donors a tangible connection to a team serving Jeffersontown every day.
