Drone as
First Responder

Get eyes on an emergency sooner — before the first ground unit may even arrive.

DFR uses a pre-positioned drone to respond to selected calls and provide trained personnel with a live aerial view. That earlier information can help responders understand what is happening, locate people and hazards, and plan a safer, more effective response.

How DFR Works

1Call Received

A qualifying call is identified for DFR response according to department policy and operational criteria.

2Drone Launches

A pre-positioned drone can launch on demand from a secure dock and navigate toward the call under authorized operation.

3Live Intelligence

Live aerial video gives authorized personnel situational awareness while officers are responding and managing the incident.

Why DFR Changes Response

DFR is designed to put useful information in responders' hands earlier. NIJ describes the model as using pre-positioned drones that can respond immediately to certain calls and often arrive before officers.

⏱
Faster Eyes on Scene

A drone can be dispatched as a call develops, giving responders an aerial perspective sooner than a traditional drone team that must travel to the scene and set up.

↟
Dock-Based Launch

Modern DFR systems can launch on demand from secure, pre-positioned docks. Some current systems advertise launch in under 20 seconds, reducing the delay associated with manual deployment.

★
Safer Approach

Live aerial information can show scene layout, people, vehicles, movement and hazards before officers arrive, supporting safer planning and better-informed decisions.

✓
More Effective Response

DFR can help responders determine what resources are needed and, in some cases, whether a ground response can be adjusted based on what the drone sees.
