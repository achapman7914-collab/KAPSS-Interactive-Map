Help bring next-generation emergency response technology to Jeffersontown.

Our Drone as First Responder (DFR) initiative can give trained personnel real-time aerial situational awareness for selected calls, helping responders assess scenes, locate people and understand changing conditions.

Be part of building a smarter, safer future.♡   SUPPORT THE DFR INITIATIVE

How DFR Works

1Call Received

A qualifying call is received and DFR can be activated according to department policy and operational criteria.

2Drone Responds

A trained operator deploys a pre-positioned drone toward the incident to provide a live aerial perspective.

3Information Supports Response

Authorized personnel use the live view to improve situational awareness as officers respond and manage the incident.

Why Earlier Information Matters

Aerial situational awareness can help trained responders better understand what is happening before and during a response.

◉
Earlier Awareness

An aerial view can provide responders with useful information about a scene before officers arrive.

⌖
Better Perspective

Overhead visibility can help responders understand locations, movement and changing conditions.

★
Support Officer Safety

Additional situational awareness can support planning and decision-making during higher-risk calls.

◎
Support Community Response

Technology can provide useful information while officers and other responders are en route.

Technology That Helps Officers See the Bigger Picture.

The goal of DFR is not to replace officers. It is to give trained responders another tool for understanding a scene and making informed decisions while they are responding.
