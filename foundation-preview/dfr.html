How Drone First Responder (DFR) Works

Critical Eyes in the Sky. Minutes That Matter.

When seconds count, DFR can give Jeffersontown responders earlier aerial intelligence so officers arrive more informed, more prepared and better able to assess risk.

1CALL RECEIVED
911 CALL RECEIVED

A dispatcher evaluates the call and determines whether a DFR response is appropriate under department policy.

2DRONE LAUNCHED
DRONE DEPLOYS IN SECONDS

A pre-positioned drone can launch from a secure dock under authorized operation and head toward the scene.

3LIVE INTELLIGENCE
DFR LIVE ●
LIVE AERIAL VIEW • JEFFERSONTOWN

Real-time aerial video can provide situational awareness while ground units are still responding.

Faster Arrival

Pre-positioned drones can launch quickly and may reach selected calls before ground units.

Officer Safety

Earlier information can help officers identify hazards and plan a safer approach.

Live Situational Awareness

Real-time aerial intelligence gives command a clearer picture of what is happening.

Better Use of Resources

DFR can help right-size a response and focus officers where they are needed most.
